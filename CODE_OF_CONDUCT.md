# Code of Conduct

We want Hashem's issue tracker, pull requests and chats to be useful and safe for everyone, whatever their country, language or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep discussions technical and on topic. Persian and English are both fine.
- Give reproducible reports and concrete feedback.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's servers, tokens, bundles or personal data.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Using the project to attack, scan or harm systems you do not own.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem privately to the maintainers via [@pdnczone on Telegram](https://t.me/pdnczone) or `security@pdnc.zone`. Reports are handled in confidence.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
